## Milestone 1 scope
Equipment: beaker 250 mL, test tube, Erlenmeyer flask, reagent bottle, dropper, thermometer, hot plate.
Chemicals: water, sodium chloride, copper sulfate pentahydrate, 0.1 M hydrochloric acid, 0.1 M sodium hydroxide.
Interactions: select, pick up, drag, place, pour, add solid, insert, mix, heat. Inspector. Command foundation with undo/redo.
First demo: water + copper sulfate mixing.
Out of scope: plugins, localization, multiplayer, advanced particles, university-level simulation, full accessibility,
content packs, mobile optimisation.
