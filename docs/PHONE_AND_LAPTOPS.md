# Phone and Laptop Notes

Chemistry Lab is designed as a 2D workbench that can scale down to smaller screens while still staying usable on laptops and desktop monitors.

## Recommended usage

- **Phones:** use the app in landscape orientation for the best visibility of the workbench, the Library, and the Inspector.
- **Laptops:** keep the window resizable and adjust it to the amount of space you have.
- **Touch input:** the project enables touch-to-mouse emulation so taps and drags can follow the same interaction flow as mouse input.

## What this helps with

- The workbench stays readable on narrower screens.
- The simulation can be opened on a laptop without needing a fixed desktop-only size.
- Basic drag-and-drop interactions remain consistent across pointer devices.

## Limitations

The app is still a 2D simulation, so very small screens will naturally feel tighter than a larger laptop or desktop display. For the cleanest classroom experience, a wider screen remains preferable.
